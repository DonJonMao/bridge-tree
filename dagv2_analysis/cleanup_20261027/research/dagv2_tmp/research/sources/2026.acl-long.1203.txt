C.4 Latency Analysis of the ACMG Module
Statistical results indicate that the ACMG module
requires an average of only 1.3, 1.2, and 1.6 LLM
calls per question on 2WikiMultiHopQA, Hot-
potQA, and MuSiQue, respectively. This demon-
strates that ACMG entails few generation steps
and infrequently resorts to document-level back-
tracking for inference. Owing to its efﬁcient
triplet-level reasoning mechanism, this cascaded
architecture incurs negligible latency overhead.
